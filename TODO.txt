✅ 2. Map PC1 and Precipitation Onto the Tree
Use Case: Visualize whether species from wetter climates tend to form clades with particular PC1 traits.

Approach:

Use phytools::contMap() or ggtree::geom_fruit() to color branches or tips by precipitation or PC1.

Generate two trees: one colored by PC1, one by precipitation, and compare.

Alternatively, use a dot plot aligned with tree tips using ggtree::facet_plot.
